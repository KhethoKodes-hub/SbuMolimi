About Sibusiso

Sibusiso Molimi.

Sibusiso Molimi is a dynamic Master of Ceremonies, Keynote Speaker, Entrepreneur, and Public Speaking Coach who partners with business professionals, executives, young leaders, and entrepreneurs to help them work through their barriers to success. Captivating, professional, relatable, persuasive, and humorous—these are just a few of the words that have been used to describe this prolific speaker.

From his humble upbringing in Soweto, Sibusiso's intelligence and undeniable talent attracted three prestigious institutions that each awarded him bursaries, setting him on his path to success. He now holds a Bachelor of Commerce in Business Management from the University of Pretoria and has completed various business communication skills courses. After spending nearly a decade working in the speaking industry with multinational companies across various industries, Sibusiso knows exactly how to be a catalyst that empowers others. His streetwise sense of humour and ingenious wit make him a well-rounded speaker suitable for any audience.
