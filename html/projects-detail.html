About this event

The Township Entrepreneurs Alliance (TEA) Kasi Business Workshops are a nationwide series hosted in partnership with Nedbank, designed to empower and inspire entrepreneurs operating in South Africa's townships. Sibusiso has served as the Master of Ceremonies across multiple workshops in this series, bringing his signature energy, entrepreneurial insight, and audience connection to each event.

Sibusiso's Role

As MC, Sibusiso's mandate goes beyond simply keeping time. He creates an environment where business owners feel seen, heard, and motivated. His deep understanding of township entrepreneurship — combined with his BCom background and years of working with multinational organisations — means he can bridge the gap between grassroots enterprise and high-level business strategy in a way that resonates authentically with every attendee.

Client Feedback

Lizzy Mogale, Managing Executive for Insights and Advisory at Nedbank's Retail and Business Banking division, praised Sibusiso's work: "You understand your audience and customise your engagements to suit them. It's a pleasure to journey with you as we impact one township at a time." This long-standing collaboration is a testament to Sibusiso's ability to deliver consistent, high-quality results across multiple events with the same client.
